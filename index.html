Client Portal & Reporting

You Should Not Have to Ask Where Your Order Is

Our inspectors work from a dedicated quality inspection app on the factory floor. Findings become a PDF report, and clients follow progress through a web portal with their own login.

  • Inspection app on site. Inspectors record findings against your standard at the point of inspection, not from memory afterwards.
  • PDF reports. Every inspection produces a documented report you can file, forward or challenge.
  • Web portal access. Live online updates with a unique user ID and password, alongside weekly status reports.

Our Vendor Panel

The Network Behind Every Order

We do not place orders with whoever is cheapest this month. Our panel is built from reputable vendors we have qualified, audited and worked with — and it is the reason we can take an order without gambling on the outcome.

01

Qualified, Not Just Listed

Every mill on the panel has been visited, audited and assessed against social, security and technical requirements before it sees a purchase order.

02

High-Volume Weaving

Greige vendors are high-volume units with in-house sizing and weaving — air jet and sulzer projectile — so capacity is real, not brokered.

03

Institutional and Retail

Most of our panel produces institutional and retail lines, which means we can place your programme regardless of order size.

04

Continuously Re-Audited

A vendor does not pass once and stay approved. Compliance is re-checked as programmes run, with corrective action planning when it slips.
