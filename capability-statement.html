Nini Corporation has provided sourcing and inspection services since 1999 to companies across Europe, the United Kingdom and North America. We represent international buyers on the ground in Karachi — not as a trading intermediary, but as the quality, compliance and delivery team inside the supply chain. Our objective is unchanged since 1999: complete peace of mind for our clients.
